# Cycle Paradox Community Content Policy

This policy forms part of the Cycle Paradox Extension API License.

## Community content

Creators may use the API to make private Extensions or distribute Extensions
free of charge under the License.

Commercial distribution, paid access and any other monetization require a
separate written agreement with Rémi Bezot.

## Ownership boundaries

Cycle Paradox, its API, extension points and generic mechanisms remain the
property of Rémi Bezot.

Creators retain rights in their independently created code, texts, artwork,
audio, characters, scenarios and other original expression. Using the API
does not transfer ownership of those Original Contributions to the publisher.

Creators receive no ownership of Cycle Paradox and no exclusive right over a
general idea, mechanic, theme or creative domain.

## Similar official and community content

A compliant free Extension will not be removed solely because official paid
content later addresses a comparable theme.

Similarity between official content and an Extension does not by itself prove
copying and creates no automatic right to compensation.

If protected original material from an Extension is substantially reused in
paid official content, that reuse requires a separate written agreement with
its creator.

This protection does not prevent removal for a licensing breach, illegality,
security risk, deception, technical incompatibility or violation of platform
rules.

## Compatibility

The game and API will evolve. Backward compatibility is not guaranteed.

Creators are responsible for declaring and maintaining the versions supported
by their Extensions. Technical evolution made in good faith is not considered
an attempt to remove competing community content.

## Attribution

Public Extensions must display:

- the Extension author's identity;
- “Compatible with Cycle Paradox”;
- “Unofficial community content”, unless officially authorized;
- “Cycle Paradox — Rémi Bezot / Bezotcorp”;
- a reference to the applicable API License and this policy.

## Commercial requests

A commercial agreement may define revenue sharing, supported platforms,
maintenance duties, pricing, duration and permitted use of names or assets.

No commercial permission exists until that agreement has been accepted in
writing by the Licensor.
